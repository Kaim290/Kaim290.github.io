Unhallowed

A dungeon crawler with procedural floors, a grid-based minimap, and unique shop and boss encounters. Every run is entirely dynamic.

Map Generation

The minimap generates according to the tile-based map. The skull is the boss room, the crown is the shop, and the trap door is the spawn room.

A bird's-eye view of the randomly generated, tile-based map.

Bosses

This clip showcases two of the project’s major boss encounters.

  • The Gravemound — A hulking creature built from tombstones, bones, and graveyard debris. My brother created the base model, while I handled weight-painting, animation integration, and AI behavior.
  • The Reaper — A skeletal warlock who uses ranged magic, AOE abilities, and teleport attacks. I created and simulated his cloak in Blender, handled the weight-painting, integrated both Mixamo and custom animations, and developed the behavior system.

Animation retargets for the Reaper. Once characters are rigged and weight painted, we retarget them mostly using animations from Mixamo.

Physics Handle

I implemented a physics-based interaction system allowing the player to pick up, move, and throw various objects—crates, furniture, and environmental props. One early limitation was that players could stand on objects they were holding, enabling unintended movement exploits.

Map Showcase

The project features two distinct environments, designed to contrast visually and give the world a sense of depth and variety:

  • A procedurally generated dungeon, built using a tile-based system and atmospheric lighting.
  • A handcrafted lush forest area, created entirely from custom Blender assets and Unreal’s foliage tools.
