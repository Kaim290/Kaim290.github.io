Hello!

Hey, I’m Kai. I’m a 23-year-old game developer and environment artist with a background in Computer Science. I love creating immersive worlds, building systems from scratch, and bringing ideas to life.

Ever since I was little, my brother and I have been making video games together. I'm an artist at heart, and I love taking the ideas in my head and turning them into something real—that's what keeps me passionate about game development.

My projects range from replicated multiplayer dungeon crawlers to narrative-driven solo horror experiences. I create all of my assets and environments from scratch, giving each project its own visual identity.

Growth is the most important thing to me. Whether it's about making games, learning new skills, or refining my craft, I'm always looking to improve and become the best version of myself.
